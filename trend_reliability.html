House

    How it is measured

    For each code and snapshot: moved = sold − same months last year (units); anticipated = last year × trend (units, trend capped at ±50%). Summing in units, big codes weigh more.

    Each figure is the median of the snapshots; the small number is how many snapshots. A snapshot needs all its months closed and at least 20 codes with the trend.

    Sold and last year come from the latest snapshot that has the code (same perimeter); the trend, from what each snapshot showed.

    Appearance

    Trend analysis

    How reliable is each trend, and how far ahead? Each trend is checked against what the codes really did against last year, at 3, 6, 9 and 12 months from the snapshot. Regular items only. Hover a cell for every snapshot.

    Reliability · like WAPE1 − Σ|moved − anticipated| ÷ Σ|moved|
    Share of what the codes moved that the trend saw coming. 100% = all of it · 0% = same as assuming no change · below 0 = misleads.
    Bias · like SPPΣ(moved − anticipated) ÷ Σ|moved|
    Errors offset. Negative = the trend promised more than happened (over-forecast) · positive = it fell short.

    6-month trends

    Last 6 closed months against the same 6 a year earlier.

    12-month trends

    Last 12 closed months against the 12 before. Sell-out goes back to 2024.M07, so a 12-month sell-out trend only exists from 2026.M01 on.
