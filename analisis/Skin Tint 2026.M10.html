How WOS enters the decision

WOS does not change the recipe; it ranks where to act.

Check before cutting: Consensus above the recipe but under 13 weeks of cover. Low stock may be what is holding sell-in down.

Supply risk: Consensus below the recipe and under 13 weeks. These shades may need more, not less.

How it was built, and open questions

How cuts enter the history

History(t) = Actuals(t) + c · Cuts(t). At c = 0% the history is actuals only; at c = 100% it is actuals plus every unit cut, which is the top of each hatched bar.

The cuts here are mostly the same orders cut again month after month: over the last 12 months they add 471.6k on top of 730.1k of actuals, so c = 100% gives a base of ~100k a month, nearly twice the 54.1k run-rate and the sell-out. Across the cut period, sell-in already covers EPOS, and the backorders were shipped in Mar–May 2026 (≈94k a month). That is why the recommendation is c = 0%: the cut demand is already inside the actuals.

Count repeated cuts once keeps only the cuts that are new each month, c · (Cuts(t) − Cuts(t−1)), taking out the re-order of last month's cut. With c = 100% it gives a base of 55.6k a month: the catch-up shipments are netted out too.

The trend is the YoY of the history over the last 6 months. Sell-out YoY is +66%, but it compares against the 2025 ramp-up; month on month EPOS is flat to falling (Jul 47.7k → Sep 36.2k), so the trend is not applied.

Questions for you

  1. Cuts. Do customers place cut orders again the next month? If yes, actuals already carry that demand (c = 0%, 60.8k a month) or, counting each cut once, 55.6k a month. If part was really lost, a small c on top is justified.
  2. September 68k sell-in against 36k sell-out: holiday load-in or a one-off? October is at 8.8k so far. If it is load-in, the proposal for Nov–Dec is probably high, not low.
  3. Seasonality. Only one year exists and it is distorted by the cuts, so the recipe is flat (s = 0%). Is there a known holiday / gift-set phasing you want on top?
  4. Jul–Aug 2027 carry a 71.7k Reasonability Adjustment each with no DA. Intended, or a leftover?
  5. WOS. Whose stock is it (our DC, customers, or both), and what cover do you target? I used 13 weeks (healthy) and 26 weeks (overstock) as cut-offs. With a target I can also take the excess stock out of the first months of the proposal.
  6. The 5% CFG that still flows is included in actuals here, as the family total. Should it be kept out?
