How it was built, and open questions

How the history is built

History(t) = Actuals(t) + c · Cuts(t). At c = 0% the history is actuals only; at c = 100% it is actuals plus every unit cut. Count repeated cuts once keeps only the cuts that are new each month, c · (Cuts(t) − Cuts(t−1)).

Demand Assumptions are left out of the history and of the future. The base is the mean of that history over the last B months (only months since the first sale count for a new code); the trend is its YoY over N months.
