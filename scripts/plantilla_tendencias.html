    Trend relevance

    Each card is one trend: what sold over the last N months against the same months a year before, for the code itself, its product line, its brand or its house, in sell-in (Actuals, empty = 0) or sell-out (EPOS, 0 or empty = no data, paired code by code). Each one is tested on the months that followed: does it point the right way, how much of it carries on, and how much does the WAPE drop when the base is multiplied by (1 + trend), capped at ±50%. Regular items only: One Shots are out of the cases and out of every group sum.

    Terms used on the cards
    Direction
    when the code moved over 10% vs last year, how often the trend had the same sign (weighted by volume)
    Corr.
    rank correlation between the trend and the real growth (1 = same order)
    Carries
    share of the trend that shows up in the real growth
    WAPE at 100%
    change in WAPE applying the full trend, in points (negative = better)
    Best weight
    the share of the trend (0–100%) with the lowest WAPE
    Helps
    starting points where the trend at 100% lowered the WAPE
    Level only
    lowers the WAPE but can't tell codes apart (correlation under 0.15): it moves every code alike
